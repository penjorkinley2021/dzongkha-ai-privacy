# Privacy Policy — Dzongkha AI

*Last updated: [fill in date when you publish this]*

## What this app is

Dzongkha AI is a language-learning assistant for the Dzongkha language of
Bhutan, built by Kinley Penjor.

## What data is collected

- **Chat messages.** What you type or speak to the tutor is sent to
  Anthropic's Claude API (via this app's own server) to generate a
  response. Messages are not used to train Anthropic's models beyond
  Anthropic's standard API data policies — see
  https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy for their terms, since your
  messages pass through their service.
- **Voice input.** If you use the microphone button, your speech is
  converted to text using your device's or browser's built-in speech
  recognition. Audio is not stored by this app and is not sent anywhere
  as audio — only the resulting text is used, the same as if you had
  typed it.
- **Chat history.** Saved locally on your own device only, so you can
  return to past conversations. It is not uploaded to any server or
  account, and is not visible to the developer. Uninstalling the app
  deletes it.
- **Settings** (tone/level preferences). Also stored locally on-device
  only, same as chat history.

## What is not collected

- No location data
- No contacts, photos, or other device data
- No advertising identifiers, no ad tracking
- No account or sign-up required to use the app

## Third parties

Messages you send are processed by Anthropic (the AI provider) solely to
generate the tutor's reply. No other third party receives your data.

## Children's privacy

This app is a general-audience educational tool and does not knowingly
collect personal information from children beyond the chat text itself,
which is not tied to any identity.

## Data retention and deletion

Chat history lives only on your device. To delete it, clear the app's data
in your device settings, or uninstall the app.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same
URL with a new "last updated" date.
